# Taskly for End Users

## What this app does

Taskly is a simple but polished to-do list app designed to help you organize tasks, stay on top of your schedule, and keep your workspace feeling calm and focused.

Whether you are managing a daily checklist, planning upcoming tasks, or keeping important items visible, this app gives you a clear and flexible workflow.

## Core user benefits

### Stay organized

You can:

- create tasks quickly
- assign tasks to custom lists
- mark tasks as complete or starred
- search through your work
- filter tasks by date

### Focus on what matters

The app includes smart views such as:

- Inbox
- Today
- Upcoming
- Starred

These views help you quickly narrow down what needs attention right now.

### Plan with dates

Tasks can be assigned due dates, and the app automatically groups them into practical buckets:

- today
- tomorrow
- this week
- later
- no date

This makes it easier to keep context around tasks without needing a heavy planning tool.

### Personalize the experience

Taskly provides personalization features like:

- custom lists
- list emojis
- wallpaper selection
- a visually clean app layout

This creates a more satisfying and personal productivity environment.

## What the app looks like

The app uses a full-screen layout with:

- a left sidebar for navigation
- a central list view for tasks
- a detail panel for active tasks
- a global email composer for task-related drafts

The design is intentionally straightforward and easy to understand, with a productivity-focused user interface.

## How it works

1. Add a task with a title and optional due date.
2. Put it into a list or smart view.
3. Mark it complete or star it for priority.
4. Use filters and search to find the right task.
5. Open the item detail panel to edit notes, links, or other information.
6. Use the email composer to draft a message based on a task.

## Privacy and local experience

Taskly stores much of the app state locally in your browser so it feels responsive and personal. It is designed as a personal productivity app rather than a complicated multi-user system.

The app keeps its experience focused on the user: simple, fast, and built around everyday planning.

## Why people use it

People choose Taskly for:

- straightforward task management
- a clean and modern interface
- daily planning support
- customization options
- quick task capture
- friendly local-first experience

## Who this is for

This app is best for users who:

- manage personal to-do lists
- want a lightweight planning tool
- prefer a clean, focused interface
- want useful task organization without heavy enterprise complexity

## Summary

Taskly is a practical productivity app for people who want a simple way to organize tasks, plan ahead, and maintain a calm digital workspace. It balances usability, personalization, and task visibility without making the experience overwhelming.
